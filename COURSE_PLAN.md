# Lessons — course plan

Lessons teach a language from zero. Cloze (the Play tab) stays the place to
drill verbs in real sentences; lessons give you the words and patterns that
make Cloze easier.

## How a lesson works

1. **Tip** — one or two plain-English sentences. No grammar jargon.
2. **New words** — each word or phrase gets its own card: picture, audio
   (normal and slow), meaning, pronunciation (Irish), a word-for-word line
   when the language builds it differently, and a short note if needed.
3. **Quick checks** — after every two new words: pick the meaning, or pick
   the picture.
4. **Practice** — build the phrase from word tiles, build the English from
   tiles, listen and pick, match the pairs, plus a little review from earlier
   lessons. Anything you get wrong comes back at the end of the lesson.
5. **Practise again** — skips the teaching cards and leans on building from
   tiles. Each lesson can earn three crowns.

**Jump here?** lets you skip ahead with a 12-question test on everything up
to that unit (three hearts on screen, plus a hidden spare after three right
in a row).

Course content lives in `src/lib/tilde/packs/<lang>/course.ts`.

## Levels

### Beginner — done (all three languages)

1. First words: hello, goodbye, please, thank you, how are you
2. About me: name, where you're from, family
3. Food & drink: café words, ordering, likes, hungry/thirsty
4. Numbers & time: 1–10, today/tomorrow/yesterday, the time
5. Out and about: places, where is…, directions, asking for help
6. Everyday verbs: I/you/he/she/we in the present; have, go, be

### Elementary — next

7. Daily routine: get up, eat, work, go to bed; times of day
8. Describing things: big/small, colours, nice/old/new (agreement explained simply)
9. Weather & seasons
10. Shopping & money: numbers 11–100, prices, sizes
11. Plans: “I'm going to…” (Spanish *voy a*, German *ich werde*, Irish future)
12. What happened yesterday: a first look at the past tense

### Intermediate — later

13. Feelings & opinions: I think, I feel, because
14. Health & the body
15. Travel: tickets, hotel, airport
16. Home & chores
17. When I was young: the “used to” past (Spanish imperfect, Irish *bhíodh*)
18. Wishes & polite requests: would / could (the conditional)

Each elementary and intermediate unit ends with a pointer to the matching
Cloze level (present → Easy, past → Medium, future/conditional → Hard), so
the two halves of the app feed each other.
